How It Began

From Family Home to Boutique Homestay<

Krishna Villa is a brand-new haveli tucked into a quiet lane of old Udaipur, built in the style of the heritage homes that line the city's winding streets. Its arches, jharokhas and central courtyard echo the Mewar tradition — while every room holds the modern comforts of today.

It was Krishnaveer who brought the vision to life — raising the walls with local artisans and craftsmen, blending old-world craftsmanship with contemporary design so guests feel the charm of a historic haveli without sacrificing modern ease.

  • Newly built · crafted by local artisans
  • Classic arches, jharokhas & a courtyard
  • Modern interiors with a Mewari soul
  • 7 boutique rooms — intimate yet spacious
