Our Story

A Newly Built Haveli, a Modern Welcome

Krishna Villa is a freshly built haveli in the heart of old Udaipur, designed in the classic Mewari style — arched doorways, carved jharokhas and a central courtyard — paired with every modern comfort today's traveller expects.

  • Newly constructed in traditional haveli style
  • Modern amenities — AC, Wi-Fi & ensuite baths
  • Curated Udaipur experiences & local guides
  • Personal care from your hosts, day and night
