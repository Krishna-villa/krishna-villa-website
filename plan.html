1. Goals of the Website

Attract Bookings

  • Drive direct bookings, not just OTAs
  • WhatsApp / phone / enquiry-form lead capture

Build Trust

  • Beautiful photos of rooms, courtyards & lake views
  • Guest testimonials & verified reviews

Tell the Story

  • Boutique, heritage-feel homestay narrative
  • Host introduction — "family welcome" experience

SEO & Visibility

  • Rank for "homestay in Udaipur"
  • Google Business, maps, structured data

4. Development Phases

  1. Content & Assets

    • Professional photo shoot (rooms, courtyard, food, views) — 25–40 images
    • Write copy: hero lines, room descriptions, host story, FAQs
    • Collect 6–8 testimonials + link to Google/TripAdvisor reviews

  2. Structure & Layout (HTML + CSS)

    • Build shared header/footer and page templates
    • Home page sections: hero, USP strip, featured rooms, experiences, testimonials
    • Responsive grid for gallery and rooms

  3. Interactivity (light JS)

    • Image lightbox for gallery
    • Sticky header, smooth scroll, mobile hamburger menu
    • Simple booking enquiry form (submit → WhatsApp/email)

  4. SEO & Local Presence

    • Meta titles/descriptions per page, Open Graph tags
    • LocalBusiness / LodgingBusiness structured data (Schema.org)
    • Google Maps embed + link to Google Business profile
    • Mobile speed optimization (image compression, lazy loading)

  5. Launch & Maintain

    • Deploy to hosting + connect domain
    • Verify with Google Search Console, submit sitemap
    • Update tariffs/availability seasonally, add new reviews/photos

5. Booking Approach

Recommended (Phase 1)

  • Enquiry form → instant WhatsApp message to host
  • Phone + email CTA on every page
  • Clear tariff table and seasonal offers

Later (Phase 2)

  • Live availability calendar
  • Online payment / advance booking widget
  • Integrate with OTA links (Airbnb, Booking.com) for cross-sell
